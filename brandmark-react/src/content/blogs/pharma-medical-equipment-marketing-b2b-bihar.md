## 4 Strategic Digital Funnels for Life Sciences Companies in Bihar

### 1. Programmatic B2B Technical SEO & Product Directories
Medical device buyers do not search for generic keywords like *"medical goods"*. They search for exact device models and clinical specifications:
- Build dedicated landing pages for each machine category (e.g., ICU Ventilators, Patient Monitors, C-Arm Machines, Biochemistry Analyzers).
- Include downloadable technical PDF brochures, electrical requirements, and clinical application guides.
- Implement Schema structured data (`Product`, `Organization`, `MedicalDevice`) so Google search results display technical specs, stock availability, and manufacturer credentials directly in search snippets.

### 2. High-Intent Google Search Ads for Institutional Procurement
When a private hospital in Purnia or a nursing home in Patna needs to replace an ultrasound transducer or bulk-order surgical consumables, they turn to Google Search.
- Target laser-focused B2B search terms: *"Surgical disposable manufacturer wholesale Bihar"*, *"Hospital bed supplier Patna"*, *"Orthopedic implant distributor North India"*.
- Negative keyword lists to filter out patient queries (*"ultrasound clinic near me"*).
- Direct call-to-action to connect directly with the Institutional Sales Director via WhatsApp or phone.

### 3. LinkedIn B2B Thought Leadership for Hospital Decision-Makers
Hospital owners, medical superintendents, and healthcare entrepreneurs are active on LinkedIn.
- Publish case studies detailing successful installations: *"How our rapid 24-hour biomedical service team resolved MRI chiller downtime for a 150-bed trauma center in Patna."*
- Showcase manufacturer authorizations, import consignments, and compliance accreditations.
